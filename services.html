Services

Strategy, operating models, business process re-engineering, digital transformation and more.

Most of our transformation work touches several of these areas at once, and areas that aren't listed here. We've set them out separately to perhaps help trigger some thoughts about how these could help you with some challenges your organisation is facing.

01

Operating Model Design

How people, processes, technology and knowledge are brought together to deliver your strategy.

An operating model is the practical answer to how an organisation actually gets things done — who does what, how decisions are made, and how work moves between teams. When ambitions grow faster than the model behind them, the strain usually shows up as duplicated effort, unclear ownership, or decisions that take longer than they should.

Things your organisation might be experiencing which suggest an operating model project could help:

  • Merging functions, or entering a new market, and unsure how the organisation should be structured as a result
  • The structure that served you well at an earlier stage is now slowing you down
  • Overreliance on a few longstanding leaders to make things work
  • Slow upskill time for new recruits, because roles and ownership aren't clearly defined
02

Business Process Re-engineering

Redesigning your core processes to deliver better outcomes for your users and your organisation.

Processes that were designed for a smaller or simpler organisation tend to accumulate exceptions, workarounds and manual steps over time. Re-engineering means going back to first principles: what is this process actually trying to achieve, and what is the simplest way to achieve it reliably.

03

Digital Transformation

Building resilient digital services that exceed end-user expectations.

Digital transformation, done well, is an operational and organisational change with a digital service as its outcome — not a technology project run in isolation from the people who will use it. We focus on the service a user experiences and the organisational change needed to deliver it well.

Things your organisation might be experiencing which suggest a digital transformation project could help:

  • You know you want to make your services available to customers digitally, but know the organisation needs some help to get ready
  • You want to digitise, but don't know which parts of your business to digitise, how, and most importantly why
  • You are getting poor feedback from customers about your current services, whether digital or not, and want to make a step-change in how well you meet the needs of your end-users
04

Organisational Strategy

Helping you break new ground as an organisation: be that higher growth, new markets, new programmes or anything else.

Ambitious growth is as much an operational question as a strategic one. A sound strategy sets out not only where and why to grow, but how the organisation will actually operate once it gets there.

Things your organisation might be experiencing which suggest an organisational strategy project could help:

  • Weighing up which markets or areas of growth to prioritise, without a clear way to compare them
  • A growth plan that looks good on paper, but no clear view of the operational foundations needed to deliver it
  • Operating in a shrinking market or facing new competition, and need to understand your options to increase revenue growth

Some of this may resonate — but your situation is specific.
