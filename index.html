Why Ruhcrest

A boutique firm, built around senior attention.

Ruhcrest is intentionally small. That means the people who design your strategy are the same people who see it through — not handed off to a delivery team once the proposal is signed.

  • Evidence-based. Recommendations are grounded in analysis, not assumptions or unsubstantiated opinions.
  • International perspective. Experience spanning UK Government, development agencies and organisations scaling in emerging markets.
  • Built to outlast us. We focus on embedding new ways of working and building our clients' own capability, so the change holds long after we've left.
