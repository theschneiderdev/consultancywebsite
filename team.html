About Tom

The person behind Ruhcrest.

Tom is a management consultant with ten years' experience in the UK, specialising in organisational strategy and public sector reform.

He began his career with Ernst & Young (EY) in London, working through Consulting to become a Senior Manager. In 2023, he joined Baringa to help establish its Government Strategy business.

In October 2025, he relocated to Kathmandu as the accompanying spouse of his wife, a British diplomat — an experience that shapes the international, cross-cultural lens Ruhcrest brings to its work.

His consulting work spans UK Government, organisational transformation, operating model design, digital transformation and market entry strategy. His clients have included UK Government departments — among them the Department for Business and Trade, the Department of Health and Social Care, and the Home Office — together with technology-enabled start-ups.

He has written extensively on improving public services, and holds a Master's degree in International Development and Development Economics from the London School of Economics and Political Science.
