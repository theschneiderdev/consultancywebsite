Services

Four disciplines, applied together.

Most transformation work touches all four of these areas at once. We've set them out separately here so it's clear what each involves — and what it doesn't.

01

Operating Model Design

Designing organisations that are fit for lofty ambitions.

An operating model is the practical answer to how an organisation actually gets things done — who does what, how decisions are made, and how work moves between teams. When ambitions grow faster than the model behind them, the strain usually shows up as duplicated effort, unclear ownership, or decisions that take longer than they should.

Typical client challenges

Organisations coming to us for this work are often growing quickly, entering new markets, or merging functions — and finding that the structure which served them well at an earlier stage is now the thing slowing them down.

How we help

We map how the organisation currently operates, identify where the model is under strain, and design a target operating model covering structure, governance, roles and ways of working — tested against the organisation's real ambitions, not a generic template.

Expected outcomes

A clear, workable operating model with defined accountabilities, and a realistic plan for moving from the current state to the target one.

02

Business Process Re-engineering

Hard-wiring simplicity, clarity and efficiency into how large organisations collaborate.

Processes that were designed for a smaller or simpler organisation tend to accumulate exceptions, workarounds and manual steps over time. Re-engineering means going back to first principles: what is this process actually trying to achieve, and what is the simplest way to achieve it reliably.

Typical client challenges

Slow handoffs between teams, processes that depend on specific individuals, and work that has grown more complex than the outcome requires.

How we help

We work with the people who run the process day to day to understand where the friction actually sits, then redesign the process around clarity and efficiency — removing unnecessary steps rather than adding new layers of control.

Expected outcomes

Processes that are simpler to run, easier to explain to someone new, and less dependent on individual expertise to function.

03

Digital Transformation

Building resilient digital services that exceed end-user expectations.

Digital transformation, done well, is an operational and organisational change with a digital service as its outcome — not a technology project run in isolation from the people who will use it. We focus on the service a user experiences and the organisational change needed to deliver it well.

Typical client challenges

Digital services that were built around internal convenience rather than user need, or transformation programmes that have stalled because the organisational change wasn't planned alongside the technical one.

How we help

We bring together service design, user research and organisational change to shape digital services that are resilient in practice — and to make sure the wider organisation is ready to support them once they launch.

Expected outcomes

Digital services that meet user needs reliably, and an organisation equipped to run and improve them after we've gone.

04

Organisational Strategy

Growth strategy, market entry, transformation strategy and programme design.

Ambitious growth is as much an operational question as a strategic one. A sound strategy sets out not only where and why to grow, but how the organisation will actually operate once it gets there.

Typical client challenges

Organisations weighing up which markets to prioritise, how to structure their entry, and what operational foundations need to be in place before they scale.

How we help

We combine market analysis with practical operating model and programme design, so the strategy we hand over comes with a credible plan for execution — not just a market assessment.

Expected outcomes

A market entry strategy grounded in evidence, with a programme design that reflects what the organisation can realistically deliver.
